## What a real checkride is

To earn a private pilot certificate, a pilot takes a **practical test**, usually called a **checkride**, with an FAA examiner. It has two parts:

1. **An oral exam**, on the ground. The examiner asks about regulations, weather, the airplane, and the cross-country flight the applicant has planned.
2. **A flight test.** The applicant flies maneuvers and procedures, and the examiner checks them against the **Airman Certification Standards** (ACS).

A checkride takes most of a day. Examiners look for safe, consistent flying and good decisions, not perfection.

:::callout{type="safety"}
This course is not flight training, and completing it isn't a pilot certificate. The capstones borrow the shape of a checkride to give your sim practice a clear goal.
:::

## The two capstones

- **C8.1 Local checkride.** One continuous flight around Livermore, from a cold-and-dark start to shutdown, covering the core maneuvers in order: takeoff, steep turns, slow flight, a power-off stall, an engine failure, a pattern and landing. The page shows each step, records the time you finish it, and may surprise you with a go-around.
- **C8.2 Cross-country capstone.** You plan a cross-country flight on the page, then fly it with sim ATC and AI traffic, land at the destination and return by a different route.

Both are scored like every other challenge: Gold, Silver or Bronze on each criterion, with the required ones needing at least Bronze to pass.

## Getting ready

1. **Re-fly your weak challenges.** Check your history for any challenge you only got Bronze on.
2. **Prepare the C8.2 plan**: a route with checkpoints, altitudes, a nav log with headings, times and fuel, airspace notes, frequencies and an alternate. Lesson 6.6 shows how.
3. **Set up the sim**: the right weather, time and aircraft, with the VFR map off.
4. **Run PAVE** on yourself and the flight.

## After the course

- **Fly online** with real people on VATSIM or PilotEdge.
- **Take a discovery flight** at a local flight school. Many offer a first lesson at a reduced price, and you'll be surprised how much you already know.
- **Keep flying the challenges** to stay sharp. Future versions of Learn-To-Fly will move on to bigger airplanes.
